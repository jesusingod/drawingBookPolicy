Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

K2K (the "Provider") provides the mobile application drawingBook (the "App") and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in connection with the App.

2. Collection and Use of Personal Information

The App does not collect, store, or share users’ personal information. Image processing and drawing data are handled only on the user’s device. The App is currently free of charge and does not collect personal information for payments.

3. Categories of Personal Information Not Collected

The App does not collect the following:

  • Personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, AAID, etc.)
  • Location information
  • Payment or purchase-related information

4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions solely for the stated purposes:

  • Camera / gallery access: to convert a photo you select into a coloring outline
  • Storage: to save finished artwork on your device

Permissions are requested when you use the related feature. If you deny a permission, basic features that do not require that permission remain available.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not provide or sell any user information to third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. All features can be used without signing in.

7. Changes to This Policy

The Provider may update this Policy when necessary. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
